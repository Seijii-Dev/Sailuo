QUICK RULES
1. Choose the variant per background: light -> transparent_black / light_bg; dark -> transparent_white / dark_bg.
2. Do not recolor beyond black/white, do not add shadow/gradient/outline.
3. Clear space at least the height of the X wordmark letter on all sides.
4. Small sizes (<=48px) MUST use 03_small_builtin_mark, not the full mark.
5. Proprietary font: use for headlines/wordmark; body text may use any neutral font of your choice.
